Why the process matters

Good concrete starts long before the truck arrives.

Access, demolition, subgrade preparation, drainage, forming, reinforcement, elevations, joint placement, surface finish and curing all shape the final result. The premium experience is knowing those details are being considered—not just the pour itself.

✓
Scope before work starts
Dimensions, use, site conditions and finish expectations are discussed early.
✓
Preparation is visible
Base, forms, access and transitions are treated as part of the finished job.
✓
Cleanup is part of the project
A professional concrete job should leave the property looking finished—not abandoned.

Project process

Four stages.
One accountable crew.

A simple process makes the work easier to understand and gives customers clear expectations from walkthrough to final cleanup.

01 · WALKTHROUGH

Scope it correctly

Review location, access, dimensions, existing concrete, drainage, finish goals and project constraints.

02 · PREP

Build the base

Demolish if needed, prepare the subgrade, form the work and establish elevations and transitions.

03 · POUR

Place with control

Coordinate placement, shape the slab, manage joints and execute the selected surface finish.

04 · FINISH

Leave it complete

Detail edges and transitions, clean the work area and communicate cure/use expectations.
