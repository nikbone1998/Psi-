PSI / Decorative / Stained

Stained concrete adds depth without hiding the material.

Color can make concrete feel warmer, richer and more integrated with surrounding finishes. Stained surfaces are especially useful when the client wants the concrete itself to remain visible as part of the design.

Finish direction

Choose the relationship between color and the rest of the space.

Stained concrete is less about picking a single color name and more about deciding how the floor or slab should work with walls, stone, wood, landscaping, furniture and natural light.

1
Color family
Warm, cool, neutral or higher-contrast direction.
2
Surface character
Existing texture and concrete variation influence the finished appearance.
3
Use + maintenance
Interior/exterior conditions and long-term use should guide finish decisions.
